Song Editor

Open song test ↗

Like the rhythm game: four lanes, time running upward. The first note is at the bottom. The bars on the left show how loud the flute is, so the dips are where it's tongued.

Click a note to pick it, drag it to move it (up and down for time, sideways for lane), and drag its top edge to make it longer or shorter. A note at least 0.42 s long is a hold in the game.

Double-click an empty spot to add a note. Delete removes the picked note.

Slides: a hold slides from its lane to the next note's lane, like in the game. Drag the white bar at its top end sideways to end it in another lane. Double-click on a hold to add a bend (a diamond) there, then drag the diamond to the lane and time you want. Delete removes a picked diamond.

Click an empty spot to move the play line there, then Space to play from it.

Keys for the picked note: ←/→ lane, ↑/↓ 10 ms later or earlier (Shift: 50 ms), [ ] shorter or longer, Shift+←/→ the lane a hold's slide ends in, P play it.

Tick Flute only on notes to hear it the way the game plays it. Any part of the flute without a note goes quiet, which shows what's missing.

Save writes the song for the game. Reload the song test tab to play it.
