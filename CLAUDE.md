# Kaitlyn's Garden Song

A canvas PWA farming/RPG game: plain JavaScript with no bundler, served as static files.
Live site: https://rgeberly.github.io/garden_song/ (GitHub Pages, deployed from `main`).
The logo says "Garden Song", without "Kaitlyn's".

## Working rules (from the user)
- Commit and push only when the user says "Yes, push" or asks to push. Push straight to `main`.
- End commit messages with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- Before each push, bump `CACHE` in `sw.js` (`kgs-vNN`). The service worker caches network-first.
- Never ask the user to paste credentials.
- Check that the Browser pane is visible and the game loop is running (`G.t` advancing) before testing. A hidden pane pauses `requestAnimationFrame`. Stop and check in if something takes long.
- The user may be playing in the Browser pane. Check `G.scene` and the editor's `changed()` before reloading. Back up `localStorage` before tests that might save, then restore it.
- In Perchance, if a bot or verification check appears, stop and hand it to the user. Never work around it.

## Running and testing
- `python serve.py` serves the game at http://localhost:8000. It's also the "game" preview in `.claude/launch.json`. Its POST API (localhost only) saves song edits.
- Add `?songtest` to the URL to list every recorded song at the music stand, with autoplay, no EXP and no time passing.
- Add `?partytest` to try big fights: nothing is saved, Kaitlyn (Lv.6) has four Ivys, and it's spring. In the console, `partyTestFight(5,'rows')` starts a fight with that many pests in that layout (`BATTLE_LAYOUTS` in `js/12-battle.js`).
- Add `?alltest` to play with everything: nothing is saved, and any game started or loaded gets Kaitlyn and all four friends at Lv.14 in the party (top to bottom: Tex, Ivy, Kaitlyn, Tiny One, Tituba), every item, gear and yard upgrade, the best rod, all sheet music and 50,000 coins (`everythingTest` in `js/12-battle.js`). The `?partytest` console helpers work there too.
- Add `&pest=<species>` to `?partytest` or `?alltest` (e.g. `?alltest&pest=raccoon`) to keep one of that pest in the garden beside Kaitlyn, at the party's level, with a new one a few seconds after each fight (`keepTestPest` in `js/06-overworld.js`).
- In `?partytest`, `ivyTest('hurt'|'recover'|'better'|'released'|'home')` jumps to a step of Ivy's story (she first appears on day 5).
- In `?partytest`, `tinyTest('egg'|'nest'|'hatch'|'worms'|'grown'|'fight')` in the console jumps to a step of Tiny One's quest.
- In `?partytest`, `tituTest('stray'|'aloof'|'fish'|'home'|'fight')` and `texTest('stuck'|'home'|'fight')` jump to steps of meeting Tituba (outside the Tipsy Toad from 6 PM, from day 11) and Tex (on the garden pool's ice in winter, from day 31). Both are in `js/20-friends.js`.
- Song editor: http://localhost:8000/tools/song-editor.html?song=NAME. It can only save while `serve.py` is running.
- There's no test suite. Verify in the Browser pane with `javascript_tool` and screenshots. `node` isn't installed; Python 3 is, with cv2, numpy, librosa and imageio_ffmpeg.
- After editing `tools/songs_data.py`, restart `serve.py`, because it imports that module.

## Layout
- `index.html` loads `js/assets.js`, then `js/data/*.js` (generated data), then `js/00-core.js` … `js/20-friends.js` in order. These are shared globals, not modules.
- `G` is the game state (`js/04-state.js`). Save keys are short (`jm`, `nm`, `sh`, …), so keep them stable.
- The party stands in five spots, top to bottom (`G.slots`, save key `ps`, Kaitlyn in the middle by default). `G.party` is who's out, in spot order; `partySlots()` keeps the two in step. Battles put each member at `PARTY_SPOTS[spot]`.
- Battle rounds: everyone picks a move top to bottom (`plan`, `B.plan`), then all act by Agility (`startActions`, `nextActor`, `runPlan`). The Pep meter (`G.pep`, save key `pp`) fills on hits; when it's full, a Pep Rally (`PEP_SONGS` in `js/01-content.js`) can be played after the last pick, as a rhythm game over its own song (a `sec` would play just a stretch of one, `bc.section`).
- Pest strength (`js/01-content.js`): `pestLevel` puts a new pest near the party's level (`partyLevel`, 0-2 below), never below its own and at most `PEST_LV_REACH` above it. `pestStats` grows its HP in step with the party's (`(L+2)/(base+2)`) and its attack and XP along `PEST_ATK_CURVE`, matched to the party's HP and defence so a pest at the party's level hits Kaitlyn for about 12-15% of her HP. Grumpy pests (`CHAMP_P`, `CHAMP`, `rollChamp`) are tougher, worth more and edged in red (`champOutline`, `champAura`). In battle, pests heal only when hurt and favour the weakest member (`pickFoeSkill`, `pickFoeTarget`), and get furious once when low (`FURY_AT`, half HP for a boss).
- The Deep Forest (`PLACES.forest`, `maps/forest_<season>`, 1152 world px square) is reached from the dark-green (0,100,40) patch on the mountain. It has one painting for now, used all year (`placeMapKey` falls back to the first season painted), with hand-traced walls and front layer (`maps/forest_spring_collision`, `maps/forest_spring_front`, made by a script from polygons, not painted by the user). Its pests roam like the mountain's but in their own list (`roamList`, `G.fstPests`, `forestPool`, `ROAM_MAX`), Bigfoot now and then (`BIGFOOT_P`). It borrows the mountain's music and battle backdrop until it has its own (`backdrops/forest_<season>`). New `ZONE_DESTS` go at the end.
- The forest pests (`where:'forest'`, Lv.11-16: wolf, turkey, toadstool, badger, owl, moose and the boss Bigfoot). Pest extras: `pack` (more of its kind join), `packBonus`, `furyAt`, a boss's `intro`/`noFlee`/`outro` lines, the `hide` move (heal and brace), and the party trouble Hushed (`hush`: no songs or skills). Rare drops that are also in `EQUIP` (Silver Fang, Claw Charm, Golden Wishbone, Moonlit Feather) are kept as gear the first time and as goods after that.
- Battle effects (FX2, `js/12-battle.js`): hits freeze for a moment (`B.stopT`), shake the scene (`quake`), make the one hit recoil and flash, and throw glowing sparks (`impactFx`). Particles are soft lights drawn with 'lighter' (`glow`, `glowAt`, `glowPath`, made from `glowImg`). Each move's look is in `drawSpellFx2` (the vines for Sprout Strike and Harvest Hymn: `drawSproutFx2`); healing is `healFx`. Blows leave a mark of what made them where they land (`drawMarks`): `ATTACK_MARK` for each member's own attack, `SKILL_MARK` for skills, `FOE_MARK` for pests' blows by their `fx`; `MARK_SCALE` sets their size. `?oldfx` in the address shows the old square-pixel effects, to compare.
- Battle text: a skill, song, item or pest special move shows its name for `NAME_MS` first (`runPlan`, `PREP_MS`); plain hits say nothing, a multi-hit gives its total, centred in `MSG_FONT`; `TURN_GAP` paces turns. Troubles show as a look and a sound instead of words (`troubleFx`, recordings `sfx/trouble <kind>.wav`, made-up sounds until then).
- The garden pool: a splash going in and out (`poolSplash`: `sfx/splash.wav` if recorded, else `fish_jump`), rings and a wake (`poolRipple`), a glowing waterline (`drawWaterline`), and her MP bar over her head (`drawMpMeter`), all in the scene's glow list drawn in `drawWorld`.
- Glow particles work in any scene: in battle `B.glow`, elsewhere the scene's own list (`glowList`, `drawSceneGlow`). The time-of-day tint covers battles and fishing too (`tintBand`).
- The HD look (Settings > Graphics, `js/21-hd.js`) covers battles and everywhere she walks: `hdField` runs over the garden, town, mountain, rooms and house (indoors: no sunbeams, softer blur), and fishing has it too. Out of battle the depth blur is a tilt-shift of the top and bottom and the bloom comes from a small copy of the view (`hdSoften`, `HD_TILT`, `HD_SMALL`, `HD_FIELD_BLOOM`); sunbeams and motes stay put on the ground as the camera moves (`hdLight`'s `ox`, `oy`). Level 5 was tuned to look like the old 1 for motes and the old 10 for the colour grade and vignette (`HD_MOTES`, `HD_GRADE`, `HD_VIGNETTE`).
- Fishing: the rod's angle sets the tension; keep it in the moving green zone (`zoneHalf`, the rod's `zone`) while reeling. Too tight for `SNAP_MS` snaps the line; too slack lets the fish run line out (`LINE_OUT`, the bar at the top). The rod bends with the tension (`drawBentRod`).
- The music stand (`askStand`, `ask.mode`): Play for EXP (once a day, sheet-music songs, takes time: `startPractice`) or Play for Practice (any song she knows: `allSongs`/`practiceSongs`, crops by `G.unlocked`, Pep Rallies, sheet music; no time, no rewards: `playForPractice`). Best scores by song key in `G.lore.songs` (`recordSong`, `songBest`), shown in Lore > Song Scores with Flute Mastery (`fluteMastery`: each song's best % of its kind's `MASTERY_PTS`, 500 for sheet music, 200 for a Pep Rally, 100 for a crop, added up).
- The mountain's summit sometimes has the season's rare flower (`SUMMIT`, `SUMMIT_FLOWER`, `summitBloom`).
- Town shops (`TOWN_SHOPS` in `js/17-places.js`, each with `tabs`) open in the garden stall's window (`openTownShop` in `js/09-menu-shop.js`). Juniper's Sell tab also has "Sell all unusable" (`uselessGoods`: nothing to eat or drink).
- The pause menu (`js/10-menu-pause.js`) opens on a main screen (`drawHomeBack`/`drawHomeText`: the party with classes `MEMBER_CLASS`, the season's week as a calendar `WEEKDAYS`, where she is, the season's icon `seasonIcon`) that stays up while moving down the tabs (`SYSTABS`). `sys.focus` is 'tabs', 'who' (picking whose Status, Skills or Equip, in party order: `memberOrder`) or 'list' (in a page). Items are sorted into `ITEM_CATS` (`itemRows`); Garden Gear switches tools and yard upgrades on and off (`G.gearOff`, `yardOn`). Tiny One's `fieldUse` songs work from the Skills page (`fieldSkill`). Play time is `G.play` (save key `pt`). Items lists are two columns (`twoCol`, `itemCell`). Crit rate shows on Status and Equip (`critBase` in `js/12-battle.js`; a battle adds `B.critUp`). Save now picks a slot (`slotSave`): another slot gets a copy and the game keeps saving in its own. Settings > Return to Home (`returnHome`) costs `HOME_COST` minutes. Lore > Memories replays the friends' scenes without their `run` steps (`MEMORIES`, `playMemory` in `js/16-story.js`).
- Equipment pictures and descriptions go through `equipPic` (a `pic` keeps a renamed item's old picture: Fang Caps, Silver Wolf Fang, Spiked Boots) and `equipDesc` ("Tex only." for one-owner gear); goods can have a `pic` too. Putting gear on plays `sfx/equip.wav` once it exists (falls back to `menu_confirm`).
- Party member ids are `kaitlyn`, `ivy`, `tiny`, `tituba` and `tex`. Saves and art file names depend on them, so keep them stable. (Ivy's id was `bling` until Nov 14, 2026; saves from before then don't carry over.)
- New `SPECIES` go at the end, because saves store plots by their index in that list.

## Art pipeline (art folder: `C:\kaitlyns-garden-song-art`)
- `python tools/build_art.py` rebuilds all sprite sheets, `js/data/art.js` and the collision images from the art folder. It's slow, and GrabCut makes some outputs differ slightly from run to run.
- `named_pics()`: a loose file is used by its file name. A subfolder that holds exactly one picture is used under the subfolder's name (the user keeps one favorite out of several versions).
- Enemies are listed in `ENEMIES`. Animated ones (AutoSprite sheets, 256 px cells) go in `enemies/<name>/<name>-<kind>-spritesheet.png`, kinds `stance`, `walk`, `attack` and an optional `attack2` (played instead of the first half the time; the HOA President has one) (`ENEMY_ANIM_KINDS`, data `ENEMY_ANIM`). Each pest's frames are a sheet of their own, `assets/sprites/enemy_<key>.webp`, loaded only while it's about (`enemySheet`): all in one would be too big for WebP and for phone memory. `ENEMY_FPS_OF` sets a pest's own speeds (the HOA President's 64-frame stance). Flyers that walk on the ground on the map but fly in battle are in `MAP_WALKERS` (the seagull). The game draws them hard-edged at the size that makes the stance's first frame the enemy's battle height. A pest with them stands in its stance in battle, plays its attack as it lunges, and walks on the map, turning left or right; its shadow keeps one ground line from its stance (`foeLine`). The still picture is still used in the bestiary. The raccoon's attack sheet has its slash trimmed out of frame 9; the untrimmed sheet is in `backup/`. Painted-in checkerboards are handled by `ENEMY_CHECKERED`, and white gaps between legs by `ENEMY_POCKETS`. Studio-photo items are cut out with `SHOP_PHOTOS` (gentler settings for pale ones). Townsfolk portraits come from `people/` and are written to `PEOPLE_PORTRAITS`.
- Walls: in `maps/<place>_<season>_collision.png` (garden, town, mountain: one per season; the house has just `maps/house_collision.png`), black is blocked and white is open. The game uses the current season's (`SEASON_WALLS`, `placeCol`, `GARDEN_COL`). The town and mountain read them on a 4-pixel grid. `<place>_collision_guide.jpg` files are only for reference.
- Doors and ways in and out: `maps/<area>_transitions.png` (town, mountain, house; the garden's is `maps/transitions.png`), patches painted over a faded copy of the area, one colour per destination (`ZONE_COLS`): green garden (34,177,76), red town (237,28,36), gold mountain (255,201,14), blue house (0,162,232), orange café (255,127,39), purple music shop (163,73,164), indigo fishing shop (63,72,204), brown bar (185,122,87). Paths to the garden, town and mountain are walked into; doors take A. She arrives in the middle of the patch for where she came from (`zoneArrival`).
- Town interiors (`ROOMS`: cafe, music, fishshop, bar): `maps/<room>` painted at 512x768 like the house, with `maps/<room>_collision.png` and `maps/<room>_transitions.png` (red: the doorway back to town). Kaitlyn and the townsfolk are drawn at `ROOM_KAIT` in them.
- Battle backdrops: `backdrops/<season>`, and `backdrops/mountain_<season>` for fights on the mountain. Where each one's ground begins is `BACK_HORIZON` in `js/12-battle.js`.
- The rhythm game's hit line and side vines: `rhythm/flute` and `rhythm/vine`. Its background is the newest of `rhythm/background 3`, `background 2`, `background`. The wooden rails (`drawWoodRail`) and the rating stamp (`drawStamp`, after `STAMP_MS`) are drawn in code.
- `PARTY_IN_GAME` in `build_art.py` lists the party members built into `party.webp`. Future members sit in `party/` until they're added. `PARTY_MOVE_NAMES` maps a member's own move names to the game's (`bite` is the attack; `special slash` and `withdraw` play for skills). Tex's `fumble` loop is him stuck on his back.
- Kaitlyn's flutes (`flutes.png` in her hand, `flutes_big.png` as the menu cursor) are built from the `shop items/` pictures listed in `FLUTES` in `build_art.py`, with mouthpieces at the top.
- Front layers (what she walks behind, drawn over everyone) are see-through pictures showing only the parts of the painting that overlap her: `maps/<season>_front.png` (garden), `maps/house_front.png`, and `maps/<place>_<season>_front.png` (town, mountain). The user edits these by hand and prefers this picture format over masks. Never overwrite them.
- Party art: loose `party/<Name>-<kind>-spritesheet.png`, or a folder `party/<Name>/` with `<Name> - <kind> - spritesheet.png` (Tiny One's). `PARTY_IDS` maps folder names to ids (`tiny one` is `tiny`). Crops' `harvest <Crop>` pictures are the bag icons; `<Tree> sapling` shop pictures replace the trees' seeds.
- Kaitlyn's battle sheet: `python tools/build_battle.py` builds `assets/sprites/battle.png` and `BATL_META` (in `js/assets.js`) from `party/Kaitlyn/battle.png` (kick, kick2, flute and victory rows) and her `karate kick` spritesheet (the `karate` row).
- Kaitlyn's expressions: `party/Kaitlyn/kaitlyn-portrait-<expression>.jpg` become `portrait_kaitlyn-<expression>` (spaces as dashes). A talk line shows one with `face:'kaitlyn-<expression>'`; the talk box falls back to her usual portrait.
- Portraits: a member's `portrait2` (or `portrait 2`) picture wins over `portrait` and keeps its whole square, so the animal looks smaller. Ivy, Tituba and Tiny One (and his baby portrait) use theirs.
- Running animation: `python tools/build_run.py` produces `assets/sprites/run.png` and `js/data/run.js`.
- Animated effects made in Effekseer: `effects/<name>.png` in the art folder, exported with the Recorder as a single image, X Count 8, square frames, "Make transparent: None" (black, drawn as added light; list ones made with "Original image" in `SOLID`). `python tools/build_effects.py` trims empty frames and writes `assets/effects/<key>.webp` and `js/data/effects.js` (`EFFECTS`). A friend's skill plays one through `SKILL_SHEET` in `js/12-battle.js`; a skill with several hits plays an equal share of the frames on each hit's pest (`sheetHit`). `SHEET_GAIN` brightens added-light sheets on the bright garden.
- Perchance prompts are in `tools/perchance-prompts.md`. Save full-resolution images with the heart icon, then "save to your device". Downloads arrive as `<uuid>.tmp` in Downloads, and the save windows are dialogs titled `data:image/...`.

## Sound effects
- Recordings are `sfx/<name>.wav` in the art folder. `python tools/build_sfx.py` cleans them up (DC offset, leading silence, fades, even loudness), writes `assets/audio/sfx/<key>.m4a` and lists them in `js/data/sounds.js` (`SOUNDS`). The key is the name in lower case with spaces and dashes as underscores. A name ending in a number ("close door 2") or a copy number ("bite (1)", "bite 1 (1)") is another take, picked at random; each sound's takes are renumbered 1, 2, 3... in the build.
- `sfx(key, vol)` in `js/03-audio.js` plays one (Web Audio, decoded at start-up). `SOUND_VOL` there sets how loud each plays.
- Settings has Music and Sound effects levels, 0 to 10 (`VOL`, `setVolume`). They're stored in `localStorage` (`kgs-vol`), not the save. Music covers the tracks and the rhythm game's recordings (`songGain`). Sound effects cover everything else, including the footstep and one-shot `<audio>` sounds.
- Sounds in `LOOPS` (`build_sfx.py`, now just `fishing_reeling`) are built to repeat without a seam and played with `sfxLoop`, which can change volume and speed while it runs.
- Menu clicks are automatic: `menuClick` in `js/05-input.js` compares the menus before and after each key or tap. If nothing played a sound, it plays `menu_tab`, `menu_confirm`, `menu_cancel` or `menu_move`.
- Kaitlyn's battle and field songs play `sfx/<song name>.wav` (`soundKey(name)`), with the music turned down meanwhile (`duckMusic`). Each plays through, then its effect lands as the song ends: in battle the move's animation runs last (`anim`'s `atEnd`), in the garden from `G.cast.fxFrom`. The clock stops during a field song.
- Battle sounds (`js/12-battle.js`): a friend's plain attack plays `ATTACK_SOUND` (kick hit, bite, slash, chomp), a pest's blow plays `FOE_HIT_SOUND` by its fx, anything else that hits plays heavy_hit; also critical_hit, miss, blocked, whoosh (lunges), focus, eat_crop, level_up. A skill with a recording named after it (`skillVoice`: Ssssss, Fury Swipes, Shell Up...) plays it, and its first hit makes no other sound. Planting, harvesting and clearing a dead plant have their own too.
- Tiny One's skill voices are in `SKILL_SOUND` (`js/12-battle.js`). A voice longer than the move holds the turn (`anim`'s `hold`) while his animation loops.

## Songs and the rhythm game
- Recordings are `rhythm/songs/<name>_flute.wav` and `<name>_backing.wav` in the art folder. Crop songs are named by species key; practice songs are `practice_<Title>`.
- `python tools/build_songs.py [names…]` converts them to `assets/audio/songs/*.m4a` and finds the notes in `js/data/songs.js`. Never chain it after other edits in the same command; it once wrote an empty song list.
- Hand edits made in the editor live in `tools/song_edits.json` and always win over the automatic note finder.
- `PARTS` in `tools/songs_data.py` defines songs made of other songs: Grow Little Croppies and Drink Up, Lovelies (crop songs), and Croppies In Your Mouth! and Not in My Pep Rally! (Pep Rally songs). `recompose()` rebuilds them, and the last note of each part keeps its own lane.
- Note format is `{t, d, s, l}` plus an optional `e` (end lane) and `b` (bends, `[[secondsIn, lane]]`). The game's hold rules are in `songHoldDur` (`js/11-rhythm.js`), and the editor's `gameDur` mirrors them.
